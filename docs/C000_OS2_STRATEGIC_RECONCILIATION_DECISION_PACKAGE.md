## Decision

Damavand Steel adopts Project OS 2.0 as the program operating model. OS2 preserves the approved enterprise truth architecture while creating the shortest safe path to bounded commercial learning.

OS2 follows:

```text
PRESERVE → RECONCILE → SIMPLIFY → EXTEND
```

It does not rewrite the platform from zero and does not convert planning records into implementation authority.

## OS2 Operating Architecture

| Track | Purpose | Source-of-Truth boundary | Primary handoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `A — Commercial Fast Track` | Bounded inquiry, feedback, and revenue learning | Consumes approved Repository truth; WordPress/WooCommerce remains a reversible downstream projection | Inquiry and UX outcomes to C; Product/data gaps to B |
| `B — Core Platform / Truth Engine` | Product Data, taxonomy, Knowledge, provenance, and governed administration | Repository owns Product and Knowledge truth | Approved bounded slices to A; canonical IDs and contracts to C/D |
| `C — Growth & Revenue` | Existing-customer, referral, SEO, channel, lead, and follow-up learning | CRM/lead tooling may own lead state only; it never owns Product truth | Commercial outcomes to A; recurring questions and gaps to B |
| `D — Automation & Scale` | Automate stable, measured processes | n8n/OpenAI may consume approved contracts but cannot own truth or approval | Starts only after A–C demonstrate repeatability |

Default WIP focus is at most:

```text
1 Commercial + 1 Core + 1 Enabler
```

The controlled feedback path is:

```text
Repository truth
→ controlled projection
→ customer interaction
→ lead outcome / data gap
→ governed review
→ Repository truth
```

Uncontrolled bidirectional synchronization is prohibited.

## Commerce Policy

Founder-approved target architecture:

```text
Inquiry First by default + future SKU-level purchase eligibility
```

Current operational mode remains inquiry-first with no active public purchase authority. No current Product may expose or enable public pricing, cart, checkout, payment, or purchase behavior.

Future purchase eligibility is fail-closed and per SKU. It requires separately approved Product identity and SKU, evidence-backed commercial availability, price authority, fulfillment, inventory, legal/commercial controls, security, reconciliation, rollback, Runtime approval, Production approval, and Founder approval. Missing or expired evidence returns the SKU to inquiry-only behavior.

This target decision does not itself supersede the operational controls of [ADR-0001](adr/0001-inquiry-first-commerce.md). A later purchase-enablement mission must record the required superseding ADR and reviews before activation.

## Pilot Correction

The three approved PD-03B Pilot records are seed/reference evidence only. They are not Products, SKUs, Availability, Master Data, Golden assets, runtime objects, or a maximum ceiling on the future Commercial Fast-Track scope.

`C002` may later define a commercially useful bounded slice from governed evidence. It must not promote Pilot identifiers to Product/SKU identity, infer availability, generate theoretical combinations, or treat the current three records as an executable assortment without separate approval.
