## 5. Evidence and score discipline

Every observation is `EXTERNAL_OBSERVATION` and includes the competitor identity,
tier, capability, domain, source URL, date, status, confidence, relevance and a
copyright-safe abstract note. Status is one of `VERIFIED`,
`PARTIALLY_VERIFIED`, `UNVERIFIED`, `STALE` or `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

The matrix uses the Mission's 0–5 scale only as an analysis aid. It contains one
record for each competitor/dimension pair (`13 × 28 = 364`). Every record has a
rationale, evidence references, confidence and date. Unsupported dimensions use
`null` with a fail-closed evidence status. No weighted total, average, ordinal
league table or scientific-precision claim is permitted.

Leadership means the strongest observed public pattern within this bounded set,
not an overall company ranking. A stale or unverified surface cannot become a
current leader.

## 6. Evidence-class separation

The advantage registry uses three non-interchangeable classes:

- `EXTERNAL_OBSERVATION`: public competitor structure or behavior;
- `FOUNDER_CONFIRMED`: an existing canonical C003/C003-R1 business fact or constraint;
- `ARCHITECTURE_PROPOSAL`: a C004 or Idea Vault planning mechanism.

External evidence cannot become Founder truth. Founder truth cannot be expanded
into a mechanism not contained in its source. An architecture proposal cannot
become implementation authority. The known `51 + 38 + 16` and approximate
`1 : 1 : 3` relationship remains historical, non-current context and never a
mandatory bundle, stock, price or compatibility rule.

## 8. Fast Track and long-term separation

`USE_NOW` means adopt the principle in current planning, not implement it. The
Fast Track view is limited to low-regret decisions that reduce discovery/inquiry
friction while preserving canonical owners. High-dependency capabilities are
`PLAN_NOW_IMPLEMENT_LATER`, `DEFER` or `NEEDS_FOUNDER_DECISION`.

No AI capability belongs to Phase 1. Calculator, CRM automation, personalization,
automatic cross-selling and supplier automation remain non-implemented.

## 9. Copyright and source boundary

observations with citations. It does not copy competitor prose, descriptions,
images, tables, branded assets or proprietary content. Public prices may be
observed as a presentation capability, but no competitor price amount is imported
