## Reading contract

Scores are bounded analysis aids, not objective rankings. `0` means an observed
unusable/absent surface; `5` means benchmark-level public evidence in this set.
`—` means the inspected evidence did not support a score. It never proves that a
private capability is absent. No total, average or league table is calculated.

Detailed score rationale and evidence references are canonical in
`repository/data/registries/extensions/c004/capability-scores.yaml`.

## Competitor findings

### Tier 1

**Steel Majlesi** — The taxonomy-breadth hypothesis is validated. Search,
filters, commercial context and trust are strong, but the deep menu and
page-per-combination approach create overload and fragmentation risk. Sources:
[home](https://steelmajlesi.com/),
[201/51 category](https://steelmajlesi.com/product-category/9000083/9000074/).

**Atropat** — Low-friction direct navigation is validated relative to Tier 1.
Its 51-mm family exposes useful alloy/thickness/brand selection, but public
evidence does not prove canonical tuple enforcement. Sources:
[home](https://atropatco.ir/),
[51-mm family](https://atropatco.ir/product/%D9%84%D9%88%D9%84%D9%87-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%DB%8C%D9%84-51-2/).

**SteelRokh** — The structured Product Data and commercial-table hypothesis is
strongly validated. It also provides the clearest Tier-1 calculator, technical
content and Product-to-Knowledge precedent. Dense tables should be adapted, not
copied into mobile UX. Sources:
[pipe category](https://steelrokh.com/product-category/%D9%84%D9%88%D9%84%D9%87-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%DB%8C%D9%84/),
[calculator](https://steelrokh.com/%D9%85%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%A8%D9%87-%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%86%DB%8C/).

**Steel Sheet** — The fittings-depth hypothesis is validated. The useful
application vocabulary is offset by unfinished descriptions, zero-price states
and thin page proliferation. Source: [shop](https://steel-sheet.ir/shop/).

**Kia Steel** — A strong fittings and variable-attribute precedent, but only a
candidate variant-UX leader because valid combinations were not exercised. Mixed
commercial states and long knowledge copy inside Product pages are anti-patterns.
Sources: [shop](https://kiasteel.com/shop/),
[pipe family](https://kiasteel.com/product/%D9%84%D9%88%D9%84%D9%87-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%DB%8C%D9%84/).

**Sam Steel Shop** — A relevant WooCommerce variable-product precedent is
partially validated.
It exposes useful unit, tolerance and fulfillment context, but combines many
separate pages with broad variations and has weaker access repeatability.
Source: [201 pipe category](https://shop.sam-steel.ir/product-category/steelpipe/loole201/).

### Tier 2 and adjacent

**Ahan1** — Candidate Tier-2 leader for breadth, filtering and crawlable
commercial data. Its extensive menu/page surface is also a fragmentation warning.
Source: [stainless pipe](https://ahan1.com/Category/pipe/steel-pipe/).

**MarkazeAhan** — Candidate leader for visible specialist routing, assisted
comparison and process/trust clarity. Source: [home](https://www.markazeahan.com/).

**Pivan** — Strong product-plus-industry discovery, calculator platform and
specialist-team routing. Its very broad scope is not a model for Damavand catalog
expansion. Sources: [home](https://pivan.co/),
[calculator hub](https://pivan.co/weight-table/).

**Iromart** — The current public root is suspended; it is excluded from all
current leadership. Historical table, calculator and assisted-order patterns are
`STALE / LOW` only. Source: [current root](https://iromart.com/).

**SteelRonic** — Compact stainless navigation, consultation and alloy-aware
calculator are useful; empty commercial tables and an unproven commerce shell are
not. Sources: [home](https://steelronic.com/),
[calculator](https://steelronic.com/steel-calculator/).

**Saba Profile** — Strong processing-service, cutting/weight tooling and
technical-content evidence. Repetitive pages, stale-price risk and warehouse-like
labels require avoidance. Source:
[calculation panel](https://sabaprofile.com/%D9%BE%D9%86%D9%84-%D9%85%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AA%DB%8C-%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%86/).

**SteelParto** — Sufficient live evidence supports Tier-3 inclusion as
`PARTIALLY_VERIFIED`. It offers a relevant fittings and variable-product
precedent, but its public API also demonstrates contradictory stock, price and
purchasability states. Source:
[public Store API](https://steelparto.com/wp-json/wc/store/v1/products?per_page=5).

## Cross-market conclusion

Competitors prove that catalog depth, commercial data, technical content,
calculators, fittings relationships and human assistance can create value. They
also repeatedly expose the same failure modes: mega-menu overload, fragmented
pages, mixed price/inquiry/cart states, stale or contradictory Availability, and
Product/Knowledge duplication. Damavand's opportunity is not to copy a site; it
is to combine the strongest principles through canonical owners and fail-closed
commercial truth.
