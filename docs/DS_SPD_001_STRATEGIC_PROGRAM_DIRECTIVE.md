• Business knowledge.
• Product Data.
• Product Knowledge.
• Product taxonomy.
• Product presentation.
• Customer inquiry.
• Lead handling.

The Damavand Steel product domain includes:
• Large and evolving product families.
• Complex product variations.
• Technical attributes.
• Differing supplier terminology.
• Differing market terminology.
• Incomplete source information.
• Uncertain availability.
• Variable commercial conditions.
• Unstable public prices.
