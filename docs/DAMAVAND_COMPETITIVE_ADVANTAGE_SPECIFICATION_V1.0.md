# Damavand Competitive Advantage Specification v1.0

**Mission:** `C004`
**Status:** Governed planning specification
**Implementation authority:** None

## 1. Strategic position

Damavand should not copy one competitor. The target is an original combination:

- data depth comparable to the strongest structured commercial surfaces;
- catalog breadth without mega-menu or page explosion;
- direct, low-friction family navigation;
- canonical Product and Variant truth;
- explicit inquiry/commercial states controlled by governed evidence;
- original technical knowledge linked to Product intent;
- Mobile First Persian RTL selection;
- visible human assistance where automation is not authorized.

`USE_NOW` means adopt the principle in architecture and planning now. It never
means implement, publish, activate commerce or bypass a later Mission gate.

## 3. Advantage contracts

### A. Unified Product Family UX — USE NOW

Customer flow:

`Product Family → Grade → Brand → Thickness → Appearance → Length`

Here, Appearance is explicitly distinct from canonical Finish, PVD and
electrostatic-coating semantics.

The sequence is a C004 architecture proposal; it is not a new Product hierarchy.
Only combinations already evidenced through the canonical hierarchy and Variant
Rules may remain selectable. The selector cannot create a Product, term, tuple,
SKU, Availability or price. It must support `UNKNOWN` and stop safely when a
choice lacks canonical evidence.

Customer value is fewer dead ends and less page hunting. Business value is a
single learnable family surface instead of manual page proliferation.

### B. Context-Aware Commercial State — PLAN NOW, IMPLEMENT LATER

A selected canonical context may eventually project:

- Mass/weight context from approved provenance;
- supply/Availability context from a future owner;
- price or inquiry state from commercial authority;
- CTA and message consistent with the selected state.

The projection must not merge Product Truth, Commercial Truth, Availability,
Price or Operator Authority. A missing input yields inquiry/verification, never an
invented value. C004 creates no projection and no active state.

### C. System Selling — PLAN NOW, IMPLEMENT LATER

Exact Founder source `C0BNHRRTE9F / 1787056479.144299` preserves
`FOUNDER_CONFIRMED / CURRENT_INTENT / EVIDENCE ONLY`: 201/51 is recurring/core;
51, 38 and 16 are usually high-demand complementary railing sizes; and an
approximate `1 : 1 : 3` consumption pattern is common. This may inform future
related-product, project-calculator and assisted-order questions. It is not
Product relationship truth, a Variant Rule, bundle, quantity rule, SKU,
Availability, stock, price formula, automatic cross-sell or universal
compatibility rule.

Future suggestions must resolve through Product `Related To`, C002 Inventory
Harmony, evidence dates and operator review. Suggestions never auto-add items or
enable commerce.

### D. Availability Trust Model — USE NOW

The market repeatedly shows false, stale or contradictory stock presentation.
Founder evidence already confirms the customer-facing business model:

- `موجود` without publishing exact inventory quantity;
- `قابل تأمین سریع` for routinely sourceable goods, subject to operator finalization;
- `نیازمند استعلام` when supply needs confirmation;
- `فعلاً ناموجود` when temporarily not orderable.

Normal fulfillment may come from own stock for the item and/or the supplier
network. A routinely sourceable item may remain orderable when own stock for that
item is absent. Operator verifies/finalizes supply, and C004 makes no categorical
warehouse-ownership assertion.

The trust/separation principle is therefore `USE_NOW` in planning. This does not
create Availability. Future implementation still requires a separately governed
Availability owner and resolution of:

- warehouse-confirmed;
- market-assured;
- verification-required.

Evidence freshness, expiry, source confidence, owner mechanics, escalation and
Runtime projection remain independently gated. Nothing here creates stock,
Availability or a customer shipping promise.

### E. Operator-Controlled Commercial Layer — USE NOW

Temporary price, rounding, exception and final commercial decisions must never
overwrite canonical Product Data. Architecture planning must preserve explicit
operator ownership, evidence, chronology and auditability. The current customer
mode remains inquiry-first; C004 creates no order, price, checkout or payment.

### F. Data-Rich but Simple UX — USE NOW

The core experience rule is:

> Preserve the strongest observed data depth and catalog coverage, but disclose
> only the next information or choice needed for the customer's current context.

Deep technical fields remain accessible. They do not all appear in the first
mobile viewport or as a giant table. Search and filtering complement, but do not
replace, the canonical dependent selector.

### G. Product + Knowledge Architecture — USE NOW

Commercial intent owners and knowledge intent owners remain distinct:

- Product family, grade/size and commercial pages help a customer select or inquire;
- comparison, application, selection, weight and engineering guidance explain;
- governed internal links move between them.

One intent gets one canonical owner. Faceted combinations are not automatically
indexable pages. Product pages do not repeat long educational copy.

### H. Technical Content Authority — USE NOW

Competitors may reveal topics, market vocabulary and gaps. Final technical content
must use authoritative sources, structured research, technical review and Founder
review where business knowledge is involved. Competitor prose, tables, images and
descriptions are never drafting sources.

### I. Mobile-First Persian RTL Industrial Commerce — USE NOW

The selection experience is designed first for a narrow Persian RTL viewport.
Grade, brand, thickness, finish, length and inquiry state must be reachable through
progressive steps with visible selected context, backtracking and clear unknown or
verification states. Responsive declarations alone do not count as mobile QA.

### J. Product Calculator Platform — PLAN NOW, IMPLEMENT LATER

Strategic calculator families may include:

- Mass/weight and meter/branch conversion;
- component usage and related-product context;
- railing/project estimation;
- protected commercial/margin support where separately authorized.

Inputs must be canonical, versioned and source-bound. A calculator cannot create
Product, Mass, price or engineering truth. Central Solution calculations remain a
future boundary and no calculator is implemented by C004.

## 4. Fast Track view

The Fast Track planning slice is deliberately small:

1. one governed Product-family entry rather than page-per-combination discovery;
2. progressive dependent selection only after future evidence is separately
   promoted into canonical Variant Rules under separate authority;
3. a compact mobile summary only after a Product context is separately selected
   and canonically promoted under separate authority;
4. clear inquiry/operator CTA with no false purchase promise;
5. separate technical guidance links for `201`, size `51`, thickness selection and application;
6. explicit missing/verification states;
7. instrumentation requirements for future learning, without activating Runtime.

This view strengthens a future `201 / 51` experience but does not populate or
select the C002 Pilot candidate.

## 5. Long-term view

Long-term, separately governed candidates are:

- context-aware commercial projection;
- calculator platform and project estimation;
- system-selling suggestions and assisted order building;
- CRM-visible specialist routing and follow-up;
- advanced personalization;
- multi-supplier and Availability automation.

There are no AI features in Phase 1. Long-term value does not grant implementation
or successor-Mission authority.
