# Damavand Competitive Anti-Pattern Register v1.0

**Mission:** `C004`
**Purpose:** What Damavand will deliberately not copy
**Authority:** Planning guardrails only

Competitor names identify a bounded observed example; they do not assert intent,
internal architecture or general company quality. Prevention rules bind future
planning to canonical Damavand owners and require separate implementation gates.

| ID | Competitor/example | Observed problem | Architectural risk | UX/business impact | Damavand prevention rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AP-01 | Steel Majlesi and other broad catalogs | The full deep taxonomy is exposed at once | Customer menu becomes a parallel hierarchy | Mobile overload and high maintenance | Keep breadth in canonical data; expose progressive, task-oriented routes |
| AP-02 | Multiple page-per-combination catalogs | Arbitrary combinations receive separate pages | Product and SEO truth duplicate across URLs | Cannibalization, stale pages and inconsistent changes | Create only unique evidence-backed intent owners; keep variants under one family |
| AP-03 | Steel Sheet | Incomplete descriptions and zero-price states are public | Publication is detached from completeness and commercial state | Trust loss and misleading action cues | Fail publication until content and commercial-state gates pass |
| AP-04 | SteelParto Store API | Stock boolean, stock text, price and purchasability disagree | Multiple projections diverge | Customers receive contradictory promises | Build one fail-closed state from separately governed Product, Availability and Commerce evidence |
| AP-05 | Saba and broad marketplaces | Old commercial values or warehouse-like labels remain visible | Stale evidence is treated as current truth | Bad quotes, false urgency and escalation | Require source, observed-at, validity and operator verification for each commercial claim |
| AP-06 | Kia and Sam mixed commerce states | Range, zero, inquiry and cart cues coexist without a state contract | UI implies authority the data lacks | Dead ends and accidental checkout expectations | Keep explicit inquiry-first and future per-SKU eligibility states |
| AP-07 | SteelRokh dense tables | The full data table becomes primary selection UI | Desktop IA is projected directly to mobile | Slow scanning and selection errors | Use progressive family selection plus accessible detailed views |
| AP-08 | Repeated Product/education mixes | Long generic guidance is repeated inside Product pages | Knowledge and Product owners collapse | Stale content and keyword cannibalization | Own knowledge once and connect it through governed internal links |
| AP-09 | Generic Woo variable catalogs | Variation controls expose combinations without canonical evidence | Runtime becomes a substitute for Variant Rules | Impossible orders and admin sprawl | Project only evidence-backed valid tuples; unknowns remain unavailable for selection |
| AP-10 | Public stock presentations | Exact stock or availability language lacks inspectable provenance | Presentation outruns supply truth | Broken promises and reputational harm | Never infer stock; require evidence, validity and operator verification |
| AP-11 | Competitor descriptions/media | Research tempts direct reuse of prose, tables or images | Copyright and provenance are lost | Legal risk and undifferentiated content | Capture abstract patterns only; create original authoritative Damavand content |
| AP-12 | Desktop-first mega-menus | Every category and choice is shown before customer context | Navigation ignores dependency order | Abandonment on mobile Persian RTL | Ask only the next valid question and preserve visible selected context |

## Additional rejection rules

- Do not create one page per thickness, brand or finish only because a competitor does.
- Do not embed temporary commercial values in Product truth.
- Do not turn the Founder-confirmed current-intent `51 : 38 : 16` consumption evidence into Product truth, a bundle, quantity rule, stock claim or automatic cross-sell.
- Do not expose controlled-value proposals as selectable values.
- Do not treat a competitor's price, stock, warehouse label or delivery claim as Damavand evidence.
- Do not use an empty cart, checkout or account shell to imply a capability.
- Do not publish thin SEO pages for every filter state.
- Do not use copied competitor descriptions, screenshots, images or calculator text.
- Do not infer backend CRM, inventory or admin quality from a public frontend.
- Do not treat C004 planning status as implementation authorization.

Machine-enforced versions of these rules are stored in
`repository/data/registries/extensions/c004/damavand-advantages.yaml`.
