# SEO Strategy

## Purpose

Define an entity-first, evidence-backed Phase-1 SEO strategy that preserves
canonical ownership, useful Persian discovery, and inquiry-first/no-public-price
boundaries without creating or publishing pages.

## Scope

This Draft covers intent governance, canonical landing ownership, indexability,
internal linking, structured-data boundaries, content/media evidence, and future
measurement. It authorizes no keyword set, page, URL, schema markup, plugin
configuration, publication, or AI search feature.

## SEO Baseline

- SEO projects only approved public projections of canonical entities; it cannot
  become Product, Taxonomy, Content, Media, Availability, or Price truth.
- Each approved intent has one canonical landing owner. Search/filter/selector
  states are non-canonical and non-indexable by default.
- Public structured data must match visible approved content and exclude Price,
  Offer, stock, unsupported claims, protected evidence, and transaction behavior.
- Mobile First, Persian RTL, accessibility, evidence-backed content, and useful
  inquiry paths are acceptance constraints.

Detailed architecture remains with [Search and Discovery](27_SEARCH_AND_DISCOVERY.md)
and [SEO Entity Model](34_SEO_ENTITY_MODEL.md).

`MISSING_AUTHORITY_INPUT` — exact input: Founder/SEO/domain approval of the initial
intent and entity scope, landing and canonical owners, indexability rules, Persian
query research, content/media evidence, schema eligibility, and measurement policy.
It is missing because current SEO models remain Review/Draft and C007 authorizes no
publication. Affected domain/document: SEO Strategy and its downstream owners. Safe
behavior without it: create no page, URL, keyword target, markup, plugin setting, or
public claim.

Original `Placeholder Sections` disposition: `RESOLVED_FROM_CANONICAL_EVIDENCE`;
campaign and publication decisions remain separately gated.
